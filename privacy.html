Gideon privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Gideon is a personal assistant used by one person, its owner, on his own computer. This policy covers the Google data it can access.

What Gideon accesses

  • Gmail (read-only, plus drafts): to find mail that needs attention and to save draft replies. Gideon cannot send, delete or change email.
  • Google Calendar (read-only): to brief the owner before meetings.
  • Basic profile (email address): to know which account is connected.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to assist the owner. It is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Sign-in credentials and working files stay on the owner's computer.
  • To write summaries and drafts, relevant message content is processed by the AI model provider the owner uses (Anthropic). It is not used to train models.

Limited Use

Gideon's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections.
